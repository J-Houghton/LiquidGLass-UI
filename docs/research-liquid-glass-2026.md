# Liquid-glass on the web, November 2026: state of the art vs. this repo

**Scope.** Five research angles (SVG displacement filters, CSS-only glass, WebGL/WebGPU lenses, libraries and demos, physics and motion) were surveyed and then re-checked in two adversarial verification passes (browser, engine, spec and performance claims; library, lineage, physics and Apple claims) against primary sources on 2026-11-05. Only claims that survived appear here; single unmeasured reports are marked "anecdotal", press-only or absence claims "unverified". Repo line references were checked against commit `217ff5a` of `modernize/headless` on 2026-11-05; the branch was receiving commits that implement §5 items while this was written, so resolve links against that commit if the working tree has moved on. The research summary this replaces was written against the late-2025 tree.

## 1. What this repo does today

The library fakes a thick glass slab over the page in one CPU pass. A 1D trace samples the bezel profile 512 times ([disp1d.ts:29](../src/core/optics/disp1d.ts#L29)), refracts a vertical ray once at the top surface with `eta = 1/IOR` ([disp1d.ts:31-54](../src/core/optics/disp1d.ts#L31-L54)), projects it over `y*bezelWidth + glassThickness`, and soft-knees grazing spikes at `max(1, thickness + bezel)` ([disp1d.ts:56-62](../src/core/optics/disp1d.ts#L56-L62)). Profiles: `convex_circle`, `convex_squircle = (1-(1-x)^4)^(1/4)`, `concave`, `lip` ([surfaces.ts:16-45](../src/core/optics/surfaces.ts#L16-L45)). The 2D pass fills a device-pixel RGBA buffer with neutral `(128,128,0,255)` ([disp2d.ts:50](../src/core/optics/disp2d.ts#L50)), walks one quadrant of the bezel band and mirrors it ([band.ts:6-9](../src/core/optics/band.ts#L6-L9)), and writes R/G = `128 + (-n * disp / maxDisp) * 127` with a Gaussian rim light in B ([disp2d.ts:65-70](../src/core/optics/disp2d.ts#L65-L70), [rim.ts:21-24](../src/core/optics/rim.ts#L21-L24)). The curve is normalised by its own maximum ([disp2d.ts:42](../src/core/optics/disp2d.ts#L42)); the filter's `scale` is the artist `displacementScale` or, with `physicalScale`, the optics' maximum ([createGlassFilter.ts:216](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L216)).

Render path: the buffer becomes a PNG blob URL ([encodeImage.ts:9-20](../src/dom/encodeImage.ts#L9-L20)); the graph is built as data ([spec.ts:18-30](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L18-L30)), mounted ([mountFilter.ts:34](../src/dom/mountFilter.ts#L34)) and applied by the consumer as `backdrop-filter: url(#id)` ([attachLiquidGlass.ts:16-18](../src/dom/attachLiquidGlass.ts#L16-L18)), `userSpaceOnUse` with `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` ([spec.ts:84-90](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L84-L90)). Stages: one `feImage`; one `feDisplacementMap`, or three at `(1-k, 1, 1+k)` × scale with `k = 0.02 × dispersion`, channel-isolated and summed ([spec.ts:119-129](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L119-L129)); Gaussian or `feTurbulence` blur ([spec.ts:135-161](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L135-L161)); rim light lifted from B by `feColorMatrix` + `feBlend screen` ([spec.ts:171-175](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L171-L175)): at most 14 primitives, each a full-size pass.

Motion: none; the only driver is `update(patch)` ([createGlassFilter.ts:254](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L254)), and nothing under `src/` uses `requestAnimationFrame`, springs or pointer events.

Much of this is new. Between the late-2025 summary and `217ff5a` the branch gained a per-handle `UrlSlot` cache keyed on geometry, DPR and rim ([mapCache.ts:7-25](../src/dom/mapCache.ts#L7-L25), [createGlassFilter.ts:126](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L126)), serialised renders ([createGlassFilter.ts:149](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L149)), a DOM-free `src/core` ([core/index.ts:1-3](../src/core/index.ts#L1-L3)), zero dependencies with Lit and the legacy components deleted ([CHANGELOG.md:39-50](../CHANGELOG.md#L39-L50), [package.json:4](../package.json#L4)) and, while this document was being written, the quadrant walk, three-pass dispersion, the baked rim, single-applied specular strength ([params.ts:59](../src/core/params.ts#L59)), `physicalScale` ([params.ts:21-25](../src/core/params.ts#L21-L25)) and `regionPadding` ([createGlassFilter.ts:31](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L31)). Still open: every geometry change re-runs the trace, band walk, PNG encode and decode; the support probe only proves `blur()` parses ([support.ts:9-11](../src/dom/support.ts#L9-L11)).

## 3. Map of approaches and trade-offs

There is no reproducible, hardware-stated frame-time measurement for any web glass technique in anything the research or verification passes fetched; every number below is architectural or a labelled anecdote.

**A. SVG displacement via `backdrop-filter: url()` (this repo).** A pre-rasterised R/G vector map loaded with `feImage` drives `feDisplacementMap` over whatever the page paints behind the element; the output is clipped to the border box including `border-radius` (processing-model step 4 of [Filter Effects 2](https://drafts.csswg.org/filter-effects-2/)). Chromium compiles the graph into a Skia `PaintFilter` and applies it in the viz compositor to the backdrop readback ([skia_renderer.cc](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/components/viz/service/display/skia_renderer.cc)); each backdrop filter is its own render surface ([property_tree_builder.cc](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/cc/trees/property_tree_builder.cc)). The map may not be derived from the backdrop: Chromium passes `feDisplacementMap` through when `in2` is tainted or "contains backdrop" because "the backdrop may contain private information" ([fe_displacement_map.cc](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/third_party/blink/renderer/platform/graphics/filters/fe_displacement_map.cc)), and the spec taints `SourceGraphic` and `BackgroundImage` unconditionally ([Filter Effects 1 §15](https://www.w3.org/TR/filter-effects-1/)). It renders only in Chromium: WebKit drops the whole `backdrop-filter` list at compositing when it contains any reference filter ([RenderLayerBacking.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WebKit/WebKit/main/Source/WebCore/rendering/RenderLayerBacking.cpp)), and Gecko ignores it when the graph "would fall back to a blob image", which `feImage` and `feDisplacementMap` both do ([bug 1961378](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1961378)). Both still parse the value, so `@supports` reports true (item 1).

**B. SVG displacement via content-side `filter: url()` or a DOM clone (the cross-engine workaround).** The same graph is applied with `filter` to pixels the component owns: the consumer's content rendered a second time, with lens subregions written onto every `[data-lens]` primitive ([samasante Glass.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/src/Glass.tsx)); the children wrapper with a `feComposite out`/`over` cut-out ([PallavAg engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts)); or a live DOM clone capped at 400 elements with `<video>` mirrored to a canvas ([vaso backdrop-copy.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/backdrop-copy.ts)). `feDisplacementMap` in `filter` "works in Chrome and Safari" and Firefox renders it on the unaccelerated blob path ([bug 1883933](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1883933)). WebKit caveats: compositor-owned elements such as `<video>` are silently unfiltered ([bug 322588](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=322588)); `filter: url()` is ignored when `backdrop-filter` is on the same element ([bug 297770](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=297770)); a region over 4096×4096 device pixels is downscaled ([ImageBuffer.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WebKit/WebKit/main/Source/WebCore/platform/graphics/ImageBuffer.cpp)). Firefox cost is anecdotal but large: one reporter saw "600%" GPU-process CPU on a `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` header vs about 1.5% in Chrome ([bug 1845890](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1845890)); glisse's source says "about 40ms a frame for content 1200 by 800 at 2x" on Safari's CPU path, anecdotal ([lens.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0kzh/glisse/main/src/internal/lens.ts)).

**C. CSS-only blur, rim and sheen.** A translucent tint, `backdrop-filter: blur() saturate()`, a 1 px border plus inset `box-shadow`s for the rim, a blended gradient sheen ([webtricks](https://dev.to/vitalbadjo/liquid-glass-css-the-apple-liquid-glass-effect-with-css-only-plus-svg-refraction-1hkd), [Pup Atlas](https://atlaspuplabs.com/blog/liquid-glass-but-in-css)); Comeau's oversized child trimmed with `mask-image` fixes edge sampling ([Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/backdrop-filter/)); masked stacked layers give progressive blur ([Devyatov](https://dev.to/devyatov/liquid-glass-on-the-web-6-ways-to-build-it-with-css-and-svg-3m07)); the best "lens" illusion is a 1.42× scaled copy of the content with coloured `drop-shadow()` fringing ([teodoravadani](https://dev.to/teodoravadani/a-liquid-glass-tab-bar-the-magnifying-lens-is-a-scaled-copy-of-the-bar-2g65)). None of the ten `backdrop-filter` functions displaces pixels ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/backdrop-filter)). `backdrop-filter` is Chrome 76, Edge 79, Safari 18 unprefixed, Firefox 103, about 97% usage ([BCD](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/main/css/properties/backdrop-filter.json), [caniuse](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Fyrd/caniuse/main/features-json/css-backdrop-filter.json)). Vendors say it costs extra rendering passes but publish no numbers ([WebKit 2015](https://webkit.org/blog/3632/introducing-backdrop-filters/), [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/backdrop-filter)); in Firefox it can defeat picture caching so "the entire page is rendered for every scroll" ([bug 1732817](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1732817)); one 2022 anecdote measured "almost 30%" scroll frame-rate loss from a blurred navbar ([vitepress#1049](https://github.com/vuejs/vitepress/issues/1049)). Apple's own Clear variant still blurs ([forum 795715](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/795715)); the gap is lensing, not frost.

**D. WebGL/WebGPU shader lens.** Every implementation re-derives the same fragment recipe: SDF, height profile across the bezel, normal from its gradient, UV offset by Snell's law or a heuristic, three samples for dispersion, Fresnel and specular lobes ([ybouane shaders.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ybouane/LiquidGlass/main/src/shaders.ts), [liquid-glass-studio](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iyinchao/liquid-glass-studio/main/src/shaders/fragment-main.glsl)). The hard part is the backdrop: (i) in-canvas content, where three.js TSL exposes `viewportSharedTexture` and `backdropNode` and `WebGPURenderer` falls back to WebGL2 ([TSL wiki](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/wiki/Three.js-Shading-Language), [WebGPURenderer.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/src/renderers/webgpu/WebGPURenderer.js)), and drei's `MeshTransmissionMaterial` offers the only two-interface approximation, a `backside` pass ([drei](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pmndrs/drei/master/src/core/MeshTransmissionMaterial.tsx)); (ii) DOM snapshots via `html-to-image` (ybouane), `html2canvas` once at init (dashersw) ([container.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js/main/container.js)) or liquidGL's own painter, whose vendor benchmark is a median 55.5 ms per full-page snapshot vs 86.3 ms for html2canvas on Chrome 150, anecdotal, no hardware stated ([liquid-gl README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/liquid-gl@2.0.1/README.md)); (iii) HTML-in-Canvas, where liquid-dom and Canvas UI (5,337 stars, MIT + Commons Clause) copy live DOM into a texture ([canvas-ui](https://github.com/DavidHDev/canvas-ui)), with read-back that excludes cross-origin content, visited-link state, spellcheck, autofill and subpixel AA ([WICG explainer](https://github.com/WICG/html-in-canvas)). The lens pass itself is small: "about 0.9 ms of GPU time a frame at 1280×720 on an M2 Max", anecdotal ([shadcn.io](https://www.shadcn.io/shaders/liquid-glass-shader)). 24% of Android WebGL2 reports have `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE` below 8192 ([web3dsurvey](https://web3dsurvey.com/webgl2/parameters/MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE)), so full-page textures must be clamped.

## 4. How each approach compares to this repo

**A. Family A vs the repo.** As of `217ff5a` the repo matches the core of the best verified Family A code: kube.io's 1D Snell trace through a squircle profile swept around a rounded rectangle ([kube.io](https://kube.io/blog/liquid-glass-css-svg)), the identical `(1-(1-x)^4)^(1/4)` with n = 1.5 in vaso ([vaso](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/optics.ts)) and hyalite ([hyalite.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/hyalite.js)), 128-neutral R/G, `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` (mandatory: `feDisplacementMap` reads `in2` in linearRGB by default, turning 128 grey into a constant phantom offset) ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Element/feDisplacementMap), [deepika](https://github.com/deepika-builds/liquid-glass)), `userSpaceOnUse` with an explicit region and opaque alpha ([spec.ts:84-90](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L84-L90), [disp2d.ts:70](../src/core/optics/disp2d.ts#L70)), the quadrant mirroring that Aave, PallavAg, samasante and hyalite use ([Aave](https://aave.com/design/building-glass-for-the-web), [displacementMap.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/displacementMap.ts)), three-pass dispersion (rizroze, PallavAg, samasante; vaso up to nine spectral samples) ([rizzytoday](https://github.com/rizzytoday/liquid-glass), [engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts), [vaso optics.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/optics.ts)) and rim light in the map's B channel lifted in-graph (Aave, PallavAg, samasante, hyalite) ([samasante displacement.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/src/displacement.ts)). What it still lacks: frost blur before displacement rather than after (PallavAg, hyalite; the repo's order is displacement, blur, rim at [spec.ts:62-66](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L62-L66)); hyalite's ring-masked post-blur that "removes staircase artefacts" and its optional fold; and a real support probe ([hyalite README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/README.md), [vaso support.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/support.ts)). The `feTurbulence` noise blur is re-synthesised on every evaluation and absent from every major 2026 library read. Cost: `spec.ts` and `support.ts`; nothing touches the public handle.

**B. Family B vs the repo.** The repo has none of it. Because the graph is already data ([spec.ts:18-30](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L18-L30)), the map and primitives are reusable; what is missing is plumbing: an API that takes the content (or a clone target), `filter` instead of `backdrop-filter`, per-move rewrites of primitive `x/y/width/height` (samasante's `updateGeometry`, PallavAg's `setAttribute("x", fx)`) so moving never rebuilds the map, a `feComposite out`/`over` cut-out ([engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts), [Glass.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/src/Glass.tsx)), a WebGL path for `<video>` on Safari, which Aave, samasante and PallavAg all carry ([bug 322588](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=322588), [Aave](https://aave.com/design/building-glass-for-the-web)), a size guard against the 4096² clamp ([ImageBuffer.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WebKit/WebKit/main/Source/WebCore/platform/graphics/ImageBuffer.cpp)), and the filter-id bump for Safari ≤26.x ([WebKit blog 27.0](https://webkit.org/blog/18325/webkit-features-for-safari-27-0/)). The clone variant adds element caps (vaso: 400), mutation re-cloning and video mirroring ([backdrop-copy.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/backdrop-copy.ts)); simple-liquid-glass's author reports that auto-cloning a page-sized ancestor "crashed iOS Safari", anecdotal ([spike doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucaperullo/simple-liquid-glass/main/docs/ios-mirror-refraction-spike.md)). samasante's Playwright suite covers Chromium and WebKit only ([BROWSERS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/BROWSERS.md)).

**C. Family C vs the repo.** The repo writes a black tint and `saturate()` next to the `url()` ([attachLiquidGlass.ts:16-18](../src/dom/attachLiquidGlass.ts#L16-L18)) and nothing else. It lacks the rim and sheen layers, Comeau's extended-backdrop trick ([Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/backdrop-filter/)), a frostier reduced-transparency variant, and above all a fallback that fires when Family A will not render, since `isLiquidGlassSupported()` returns true wherever `blur()` parses ([support.ts:9-11](../src/dom/support.ts#L9-L11)), Safari and Firefox included. Cost: a style preset in `getStyle()`; no core work.

**D. Family D vs the repo.** The repo has no renderer other than SVG. Its optics port directly: Hla-aung's engine is the same 1D Snell profile painted into R/G and specular textures for WebGL2 ([engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hla-aung/liquid-glass/master/liquid-glass/engine.ts)), so `displacementMap()` output could feed a shader unchanged. What the best Family D code has that SVG cannot: lensing outside the border box, per-channel IOR, Fresnel and multi-lobe specular, an unclipped backdrop, at the price of capture. The live-DOM option is Chromium 157+ only ([chromestatus](https://chromestatus.com/api/v0/features/5172548013916160)) and liquid-dom has two npm releases and no commits since 2026-09-06 ([repo](https://github.com/AndrewPrifer/liquid-dom)). Snapshots work everywhere but drop CSS animations, `position: fixed` and cross-origin images ([liquidGL](https://github.com/naughtyduk/liquidGL)); ybouane's capture needs CORS-clean fonts, taints the whole root on failure and takes "100 to 500 ms" to init, anecdotal ([ybouane](https://github.com/ybouane/LiquidGlass)). Cost: a second renderer plus a capture and invalidation pipeline; the plan's handle shape is renderer-neutral, so the API would survive.

### 4.5 Motion models

The repo simulates nothing. Verified models in the field, with reference constants:

- **Closed-form damped spring.** Apple's `Spring(duration:bounce:)` documents `Spring(duration: 0.5, bounce: 0.3)` as (mass 1.0, stiffness 157.9, damping 17.6) and `Spring(mass: 1, stiffness: 100, damping: 10)` as (duration 0.63, bounce 0.5) ([Apple Spring](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/spring)); `k = (2π/T)²`, `c = 2(1-b)√(km)` reproduces all four numbers but is a derivation, not Apple's text. Motion (framer-motion 12.7.3) solves the three damping branches analytically, estimates velocity by a 5 ms finite difference, maps `ω = 2π/(visualDuration*1.2)` and `damping = 2*clamp(0.05,1,1-bounce)*√stiffness`, and serialises to `linear()` ([spring/index.mjs](https://unpkg.com/framer-motion@12.7.3/dist/es/animation/generators/spring/index.mjs)). glisse ships the same with over/critical/under-damped branches and snaps under `prefersReducedMotion()` ([spring.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0kzh/glisse/main/src/internal/spring.ts)).
- **Semi-implicit Euler spring.** react-spring steps at fixed 1 ms sub-steps (`numSteps = ceil(dt/1)`) ([SpringValue.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pmndrs/react-spring/main/packages/core/src/SpringValue.ts)); clayharmon uses k 340, c 26, sub-steps of at most 1/50 s and clamps render dt to 0.064 s ([spring.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clayharmon/webgl-liquid-glass/main/src/spring.ts)); LiquidGlassJS runs six springs per surface (scaleX/Y, tx/ty, stretchX/Y), press target 1.045, release stiffness 300 with `damping = 8 + (1-wobble)*20`, rest at |Δ| < 0.0005 and |v| < 0.005, dt clamped to 1/30 s ([spring.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Surdeddd/liquidglassjs/main/packages/core/src/physics/spring.ts)). Velocity is state, so interruption is free; stability needs sub-stepping or clamping.
- **Velocity-driven squash/stretch.** hi-slava: per-event EMA `v = 0.15*raw + 0.85*v`, `mag = min(1, |v|/32)`, decay `v *= exp(-6*dt)`, stretch `1 + mag*|dir|*1.0`, superellipse exponent `p = mix(3, 2, mag)`, loop sleeps at `mag ≤ 0.001` ([index.html](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hi-slava/liquid-glass/main/index.html)); clayharmon: EMA α 0.25, `skew.target = velocity*0.6` via a (150, 7) spring, rendered skew clamped to ±14°, `scaleX = 1 + bulge*0.12 + min(|skew|*0.005, 0.2)`, `scaleY = 1 + bulge*0.55 - min(|skew|*0.003, 0.12)`, bulge target 1 on pointerdown via a (260, 16) spring ([LiquidGlassNav.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clayharmon/webgl-liquid-glass/main/src/LiquidGlassNav.tsx)); the Oct-2026 tab bar: `useSpring` stiffness 380 / damping 26, `width = 66 + min(92, v/9)`, vanilla dt capped at 0.032 s ([teodoravadani](https://dev.to/teodoravadani/a-liquid-glass-tab-bar-the-magnifying-lens-is-a-scaled-copy-of-the-bar-2g65)). All three hard-cap the deformation. rdev's position-only "elasticity" tracks no velocity and tops out near 4.5% stretch at defaults ([index.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdev/liquid-glass-react/master/src/index.tsx)).
- **Merging.** The SVG goo filter (`feGaussianBlur stdDeviation=8`, `feColorMatrix` alpha `A*18-7`, `feComposite atop`, 500 ms CSS transition, `transition-none` under reduced motion) is declarative but cannot refract ([tfe tab bar](https://tfe-svelte-templates.vercel.app/liquidtabbar)); SDF smooth-min fields merge refracting lenses (vaso's `field.ts`, liquid-glass-studio, LiquidGlassJS with `mergeStrength` 30 px and at most 8 lenses per group) ([vaso field.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/field.ts), [liquidglassjs](https://github.com/Surdeddd/liquidglassjs)). Apple's equivalent is `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)` ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views)).
- **Ripples and fluids.** glass-ripple solves a 2D wave equation at quarter resolution (`v += c²·∇²h; v *= damping; h += v; h *= damping`, damping 0.8, one step per frame) ([shaders.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZenAlexa/glass-ripple/main/src/shaders.ts)); Dobryakov's Stable Fluids runs at SIM_RESOLUTION 128 with 20 Jacobi iterations and `dt = min(dt, 1/60)` ([script.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation/master/script.js)), fewer than the 40 to 80 GPU Gems recommends ([GPU Gems ch. 38](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-vi-beyond-triangles/chapter-38-fast-fluid-dynamics-simulation-gpu)). Every verified use treats the simulation as the backdrop the glass refracts, not as the glass.
- **Reduced motion.** Apple: Reduce Motion "disables any elastic properties" ([WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)); the HIG lists "Tightening animation springs to reduce bounce effects" ([HIG Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility)). CSS `linear()` springs are Baseline since December 2023 (Chrome 113, Firefox 112, Safari 17.2) ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/easing-function/linear)) but "CSS still requires a fixed duration" ([Chrome guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/modern-web-guidance/guides/ui-behaviors/physics-based-easing)) and cannot carry velocity across an interruption ([Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/animation/linear-timing-function/)).

## 5. Ranked list of concrete changes

Items carry a global rank (1 = most valuable); the subsections only group them by where the change lands. Tags are my judgement on the evidence above, not a global recommendation. Items 2, 3, 6, 8 and 10 landed on the branch between `15611cb` and `217ff5a` while this was being written; they stay in the list, marked as such, so the ranking and the evidence behind them are complete. Where an item matches a Phase 5 placeholder in `docs/modernization-plan.md`, the step is named.

### 5.1 src/core (optics + motion, headless)

**3. Quadrant-symmetric band walk.** Tag: `clear win`. Landed in `7b414a1` ([band.ts:6-9](../src/core/optics/band.ts#L6-L9)) after `d2f8b2b` moved sampling to pixel centres so corner and edge normals agree. Compute one quadrant and emit four pixels with mirrored normals, as Aave ("We compute only the top-left quadrant and write each pixel into all four"), PallavAg, samasante and hyalite do ([Aave](https://aave.com/design/building-glass-for-the-web), [displacementMap.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/displacementMap.ts)); hyalite applies it only when all four corner radii are equal, always true for the repo today. It cuts the map's per-pixel work by three quarters. Remaining: eyeball the mirror axis in the demo and keep the regenerated fixtures.

**5. Spring plus velocity-squash motion module.** Tag: `trade-off, my call`. Add a pure `spring()` (closed-form, three damping branches, Motion's and Apple's `duration`/`bounce` parameterisation) and a `squashFromVelocity()` that turns a smoothed pointer velocity into scaleX/scaleY/skew with hard caps, the caller supplying time; no `requestAnimationFrame` in core. For: every 2026 library that reads as "liquid" has exactly this ([glisse spring.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0kzh/glisse/main/src/internal/spring.ts), [LiquidGlassJS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Surdeddd/liquidglassjs/main/packages/core/src/physics/spring.ts)), and closed form is frame-rate independent and serialises to `linear()` (§4.5). Against: scope creep, consumers may already use Motion or react-spring, and a semi-implicit Euler integrator fits continuous forcing during a drag better ([SpringValue.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pmndrs/react-spring/main/packages/core/src/SpringValue.ts)).

**6. Keep the physical displacement magnitude as an option.** Tag: `trade-off, my call`. Landed as `physicalScale` in `217ff5a` ([params.ts:21-25](../src/core/params.ts#L21-L25), [createGlassFilter.ts:216](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L216)), matching kube.io, which normalises the curve and sets `scale` to its maximum so IOR and thickness set on-screen strength ([kube.io](https://kube.io/blog/liquid-glass-css-svg)); vaso and glisse use the same Snell profile ([vaso optics.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/optics.ts), [refraction.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0kzh/glisse/main/src/internal/refraction.ts)). The artist `displacementScale` stays the default so presets survive (plan OQ-10). Still open: hyalite's fold (slope > 1) is its author's "liquid" signature and the Flutter renderer says Apple's rim also mirrors ([flutter_liquid_glass README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/whynotmake-it/flutter_liquid_glass/main/packages/liquid_glass_renderer/README.md)); the knee limiter ([disp1d.ts:61-62](../src/core/optics/disp1d.ts#L61-L62)) prevents folding, so exposing the knee is the cheap way to allow it. Nobody on the web traces an exit interface; keep the single refraction.

**9. SDF-driven geometry field for shapes and corners.** Tag: `trade-off, my call` (plan 5.4). The 1D curve stays; what changes is the source of distance and normal: evaluate the profile on an SDF distance instead of the rounded-rect band walk, which is how hyalite (per-corner radii), liquid-glass-studio (superellipse corners via `superellipseCornerSDF`) and vaso (`Field` grid with `smin`) get arbitrary outlines ([hyalite README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/README.md), [sdf.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iyinchao/liquid-glass-studio/main/src/shaders/lib/sdf.glsl), [vaso field.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/field.ts)). Prerequisite for items 13 and 14. Cost: a new geometry input type on `displacementMap`, slower per pixel than the band walk, new fixtures.

**12. Squircle bezel profile.** Tag: `clear win`. No change: `convex_squircle = (1-(1-x)^4)^(1/4)` is already the default ([surfaces.ts:21](../src/core/optics/surfaces.ts#L21), [params.ts:49](../src/core/params.ts#L49)) and is the exact formula kube.io, vaso and hyalite use ([kube.io](https://kube.io/blog/liquid-glass-css-svg)). The only action is documentation: name kube.io as the reference profile. Superellipse *corners* are item 9, not this.

**14. smin / goo merging.** Tag: `not worth it` (now). Apple's morphing is container-gated ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views)); on the web the choices are the SVG goo filter, which destroys anti-aliasing and cannot refract ([tfe tab bar](https://tfe-svelte-templates.vercel.app/liquidtabbar)), or a smooth-min field that needs item 9 and either per-frame map regeneration on the PNG path or a WebGL overlay with a lens cap (LiquidGlassJS: 8) ([liquidglassjs](https://github.com/Surdeddd/liquidglassjs)). Revisit after item 9 lands and only if a tab-bar or segmented-control product asks for it.

### 5.2 Rendering layer

**1. Real support detection: engine check plus runtime probe.** Tag: `clear win` (plan 5.1). `isLiquidGlassSupported()` returns true wherever `blur()` parses ([support.ts:9-11](../src/dom/support.ts#L9-L11)), Safari and Firefox included. `CSS.supports('backdrop-filter','url(#x)')` cannot help: WebKit drops the entire list at compositing when it contains a reference filter ([RenderLayerBacking.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WebKit/WebKit/main/Source/WebCore/rendering/RenderLayerBacking.cpp)), Gecko ignores blob-path graphs ([bug 1961378](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1961378)), and support is defined at parse level ([CSS Conditional 3](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-conditional-3/)). Do what vaso and hyalite do: require a Chromium engine (`navigator.userAgentData` brands, UA fallback) and `CSS.supports` ([vaso support.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/support.ts), [hyalite README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/README.md)), and gate styles on a runtime attribute set only after the `<svg>` is in the DOM to avoid a transparent flash ([dimonb19a](https://dev.to/dimonb19a/liquid-glass-needs-a-ladder-394j)). It is a sniff; revisit if WebKit's PRs land.

**2. Replace the 16-primitive chromatic stage with three displacement passes.** Tag: `clear win` (plan 5.3). Landed in `217ff5a`: three `feDisplacementMap` taps at `(1-k, 1, 1+k)` × scale with `k = 0.02 × dispersion`, channel-isolated with `feColorMatrix` and summed with arithmetic `feComposite`, eight primitives, dropped entirely at `dispersion: 0` ([spec.ts:119-129](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L119-L129), [spec.ts:62](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L62)). That is the field standard: rizroze at +0/+10/+20 with `feBlend screen`, PallavAg at `s*(1+0.2c)`, `s*(1+0.1c)`, `s` with arithmetic `k2=k3=1`, samasante likewise ([rizzytoday](https://github.com/rizzytoday/liquid-glass), [engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts), [Glass.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/src/Glass.tsx)); vaso extends it to nine spectral samples with `feComposite lighter` ([vaso](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/optics.ts)). Cost: three full-size displacement samples instead of one; hyalite warns "The three-pass dispersion sum is only valid for opaque sources", which a backdrop is ([hyalite README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/README.md)). Remaining: a visual check of the symmetric 0.02 spread against PallavAg's asymmetric 0.1/0.2 one.

**4. Fallback strategy for Safari and Firefox.** Tag: `trade-off, my call` (plan 5.1). Three verified options. (i) CSS-only frost when item 1 says no: what quickLiquid, deepika, hyalite and rizzytoday ship ([quickLiquid](https://github.com/amarnath3003/quickLiquid), [deepika](https://github.com/deepika-builds/liquid-glass)); cheapest, no refraction outside Chromium. (ii) Content-side `filter: url()` on consumer-supplied content, refracting in all three engines ([bug 1883933](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1883933)) at the cost of an API that takes the content, subregion plumbing, no Safari `<video>` ([bug 322588](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=322588)), the 4096² clamp, Firefox's CPU path (anecdotal 600% CPU in [bug 1845890](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1845890)) and, for Safari ≤26.x, the id bump ([WebKit blog 27.0](https://webkit.org/blog/18325/webkit-features-for-safari-27-0/)). (iii) A DOM clone of the backdrop (vaso, ≤400 elements) ([backdrop-copy.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/backdrop-copy.ts)): zero-config API, most fragile (anecdotal iOS crash on page-sized clones). The decision is API shape and support surface, not optics. My lean is (i) now with (ii) opt-in, but the evidence does not force it.

**7. Reduced motion and reduced transparency.** Tag: `clear win`. Apple's contract: Reduce Motion "disables any elastic properties", Reduce Transparency makes glass "frostier and obscures more of the content behind it" ([WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)). `prefers-reduced-motion` is Baseline since 2020 ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion)); `prefers-reduced-transparency` is Chrome 118, flag-only in Firefox and unsupported in Safari ([BCD](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/main/css/at-rules/media.json)), so Apple's own Reduce Transparency users are undetectable in Safari. Ship a `reducedTransparency` option settable from the consumer's own toggle as well as the media query, and have any motion module (item 5) snap to target under reduced motion the way clayharmon's `snapSpring` and glisse do ([LiquidGlassNav.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clayharmon/webgl-liquid-glass/main/src/LiquidGlassNav.tsx), [glisse spring.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0kzh/glisse/main/src/internal/spring.ts)). Cheap and additive.

**8. Filter gotchas audit: `color-interpolation-filters`, premultiply, filter region, id rotation.** Tag: `clear win`. Mostly landed in `217ff5a`. `sRGB` and `userSpaceOnUse` stay ([spec.ts:84-90](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L84-L90)); the spec applies the property only to `in2` ([Filter Effects 1 §9.11](https://www.w3.org/TR/filter-effects-1/)). Premultiply: the one map is opaque ([disp2d.ts:70](../src/core/optics/disp2d.ts#L70)) and the second `feImage`, whose alpha equalled its grey, is gone: rim light rides in B and is lifted by one `feColorMatrix` + `feBlend screen`, as Aave, PallavAg, samasante and hyalite do ([spec.ts:171-175](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L171-L175), [samasante displacement.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samasante/liquid-glass/main/src/displacement.ts)), so the practitioner-reported Chromium pitfall of alpha-0 pixels losing RGB on decode (anecdotal, [emit-solution](https://emit-solution.com/en/blog/recreate-apple-liquid-glass-css)) has no surface left; PallavAg's `feFlood` under the map is the in-graph fix if one reappears ([engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts)). Region: `regionPadding` widens the filter region ([createGlassFilter.ts:31](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L31), [spec.ts:37-40](../src/core/filters/spec.ts#L37-L40)), which kube.io's concave profiles need ("push rays outside the glass") and rizroze does by default ([kube.io](https://kube.io/blog/liquid-glass-css-svg), [rizzytoday](https://github.com/rizzytoday/liquid-glass)); the output is still clipped to the border box ([Filter Effects 2](https://drafts.csswg.org/filter-effects-2/)). Id rotation: only relevant to Family B on Safari ≤26.x; nothing to do on Family A.

**10. Per-handle cache and zero dependencies: already landed; finish the job.** Tag: `clear win`. The per-instance geometry-keyed cache exists (`UrlSlot` per handle; geometry, DPR and rim in the key) ([mapCache.ts:7-25](../src/dom/mapCache.ts#L7-L25), [createGlassFilter.ts:126](../src/dom/createGlassFilter.ts#L126)), the shared cache and the `canvas` package are gone ([CHANGELOG.md:39-50](../CHANGELOG.md#L39-L50)), and the package declares only devDependencies ([package.json:37](../package.json#L37)). Remaining: share identical maps across handles as quickLiquid (refcounted) and vaso (keyed by `w:h:radius:bezel`) do ([quickLiquid](https://github.com/amarnath3003/quickLiquid), [vaso optics.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huozhi/vaso/main/src/core/optics.ts)); use the `encodeImage` seam for SSR (kube.io's author: the map "can be pre-rendered (at build time or on the backend)") ([HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45174297)), since `filterSpecToSvg` exists ([core/index.ts:34](../src/core/index.ts#L34)). Worker-side generation (plan 5.5) is `not worth it` yet: every library read builds maps on the main thread, hyalite throttles rebuilds to once per 90 ms instead ([hyalite README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass/main/README.md)); `OffscreenCanvas` (Chrome 69, Firefox 105, Safari 17) is available if that changes ([caniuse](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Fyrd/caniuse/main/features-json/offscreencanvas.json)).

**11. HTML-in-Canvas / WebGPU renderer.** Tag: `not worth it` (now; plan 5.2). The only route to lensing beyond the border box and per-pixel lighting, now a stable, flag-free API in Chrome 157 ([Chrome blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/html-in-canvas)). But it is Chromium-only like Family A (Safari "Positive", unshipped; Firefox "No signal") ([chromestatus](https://chromestatus.com/api/v0/features/5172548013916160)), its read-back omits visited links, spellcheck, autofill and subpixel AA ([WICG](https://github.com/WICG/html-in-canvas)), and the reference libraries are young (§3D). WebGL2 suffices for the shader, so WebGPU buys nothing specific. Keep the handle renderer-neutral; revisit if WebKit ships.

**13. `corner-shape` for the Chromium path and the CSS fallback.** Tag: `trade-off, my call`. One line (`corner-shape: squircle` behind `@supports`) gives superellipse outlines in Chrome 139+ ([release notes](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/139)), and `backdrop-filter` and `overflow` clipping follow the shape ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/corner-shape)); Chrome renders it as "a single cubic Bezier curve" per half corner, a closed form "essential for performance" ([Chrome blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/implementing-corner-shape)). Safari and Firefox are preview-only ([BCD](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/main/css/properties/corner-shape.json)). The catch: the repo's map assumes circular corners, so the clip and the refraction would disagree at the corners until item 9 lands. Cheap and Chromium-only, which is where Family A already lives.

## 6. What could not be verified

- "Chromium fixed `overflow: hidden` clipping of an oversized backdrop child in Chrome 142": Josh Comeau's report only; no Chromium bug, release note or chromestatus entry corroborates it ([Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/backdrop-filter/), [Chrome 142 notes](https://developer.chrome.com/release-notes/142)).
- The "40% GPU in Chromium vs under 20% in Firefox" numbers attributed to the kube.io HN thread are not in the thread; the other quotes ("judder", "butter", "pretty slow right now") are ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45174297)).
- The iOS 26.1 Clear/Tinted setting is press-sourced; no Apple support page was found ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/20/ios-26-1-liquid-glass-toggle/)).
- Whether the Safari filter-id bump is still needed on 27.x: Safari 27.0 says the invalidation bug is fixed, but nobody cited has tested `feImage` href churn on 27 ([WebKit blog](https://webkit.org/blog/18325/webkit-features-for-safari-27-0/)).
- The Skia issue number hyalite cites for nearest-neighbour sampling is login-walled; the `kNearest` constant was confirmed from Skia source instead ([SkDisplacementMapImageFilter.cpp](https://skia.googlesource.com/skia/+/main/src/effects/imagefilters/SkDisplacementMapImageFilter.cpp)).
- Two Chromium behaviours reported by one practitioner post only: content "mirrored" back at the clip for reference filters (the spec mandates mirror padding only for `blur()`), and PNG colour premultiplied by alpha on decode so alpha-0 map pixels lose RGB (Blink has both alpha options; the default was not verified) ([emit-solution](https://emit-solution.com/en/blog/recreate-apple-liquid-glass-css), [Filter Effects 2](https://drafts.csswg.org/filter-effects-2/)).
- "Chromium caches filter definitions by id": single author post, no engine evidence; every other source attributes the cache to Safari only.
- iOS WebKit "misplaces filter primitive subregions in objectBoundingBox units": PallavAg's code comment only, no WebKit bug ([engine.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PallavAg/liquid-glass-web-react/main/src/core/engine.ts)).
- Whether landing Firefox's `feDisplacementMap` shader (D334808) alone would unblock data-URL maps in `backdrop-filter`: `feImage` has no patch and Mozilla says both primitives "are not implemented fully" ([bug 1972363](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1972363)).
- hyalite's "320k pixel" map cap, Canvas UI's "8192 px / 16 megapixel" capture caps and snapDOM's "35 ms vs 3 ms" figures: not present in the respective READMEs or source.
- Any 16-bit or two-channel precision trick for `feDisplacementMap`: unverified absence; none was found in any implementation or browser document read.
- Exactly when `url()` became usable in Chromium's `backdrop-filter`: "since M76" is an inference from the explainer and a 2019 WPT test, not a dated commit ([explainer](https://github.com/mfreed7/backdrop-filter-feature)).
- Edge cells in the matrix for reference filters and HTML-in-Canvas are inferred from Chromium, not separately confirmed.
